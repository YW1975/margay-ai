# 03 Your daily rhythm

All of these messages are sent by the background program to **your IM private chat** (the one you connected during onboarding; if you've connected several, the most recently paired one). They're sent even when no Staff session is open.

## Morning brief (every morning)

- **How to turn it on**: during onboarding it asks "What time each morning should I send today's plan?" To change it later, say "Send the morning brief at 9 instead."
- **What's in it**:
  - 【今天的安排】 (Today's schedule): items from the calendar (`15:00–16:00 见老卢（望京）`, i.e. "meet Lao Lu (Wangjing)") and today's reminders, in time order;
  - 【今日待办】 (Today's to-dos): due today, overdue, or without a decided next step;
  - 【离目标还有多远】 (How far from your goals): goal progress, with anything falling behind flagged;
  - 【交出去的事】 (Handed off): work given to other assistants; past the agreed time it says "N days overdue";
  - 【等您批】 (Awaiting your approval): you can approve right in the private chat (see chapter 02);
  - 【昨天群里谁找过我】 (Who looked for me in groups yesterday).
- **No push if you've already asked**: if on a given day you ask "What's on today?" before the morning brief time, it won't push the brief that day.

## Timed reminders

- "**Remind me to call Lao Wang back tomorrow at 10**", "**Ping me in half an hour**", "**Remind me to drink water at 10 every day**", "**Remind me to hand in my weekly report every Monday at 9**".
- When the time comes, it messages you privately (possibly a few minutes late). Say "Never mind" to stop it.
- If it can't be sent, the background program **tries again in the next round** and notes how late it is; it also catches up when you next speak to it in the private chat that day. After that day, it's dropped.
- If the time was missed (for example, the background program was stopped), it tells you privately "**Missed a reminder: X (originally MM-DD HH:MM)**", so it doesn't just quietly disappear.

> Reminders are always sent by Staff's background program, **not the host's built-in timer tools**. Those only live inside the current session and vanish when the session closes.

## Pre-meeting reminders

For items in the calendar (see chapter 04), it messages you privately once, **15 minutes before the start**: "In 15 minutes: meet Lao Lu (Wangjing)".

- You can change how early ("Remind me 10 minutes before meetings"). All-day items get no reminder by default.
- If the time changes, it reminds you again at the new time.

## End-of-day summary (every evening)

- **How to turn it on**: say "**Send me an end-of-day summary at 18:00 every day**". If no time is set, it isn't sent.
- **What's in it**: 【今天做完的】 (Done today), 【还没做完的】 (Not done yet: due today and not finished, plus overdue), 【交出去的事】 (Handed off), 【明天】 (Tomorrow), 【要注意】 (Watch out). Empty sections are left out; one summary per day.
- To check any time: "**How did today go?**", "What did I get done today?"
- Note: "Done today" means "completed, and touched today," so something finished yesterday and edited again today is counted too.

## On-the-spot alerts when something is about to go wrong

The background program looks things over every round, and when it finds any of the following, it **immediately messages you privately** "Watch out: …":

- Something you promised someone is past the agreed time and still not done;
- Several deadlines land at the same time;
- Work handed to another assistant is past the agreed time with no word back (it asks whether you'd like it to follow up).

Rules:

- **It mentions the same thing only once a day**; if it's still unresolved the next day, it mentions it again.
- **Nothing during the quiet hours at night** (22:00–08:00 by default; you can change this). It waits until the quiet hours end.
- Items that haven't moved in a long time only go into the end-of-day summary; it won't interrupt you about them.
- Ask "**Any risks lately?**" or "Which things are going to go wrong?" any time, and it lists them for you.
