# Staff User Guide

> Staff is your personal assistant: it knows what you have going on, moves things forward even when you haven't asked, stays within its boundaries, and keeps a record of what it does.
> This guide is written for **the person who uses Staff** (called "you" or "the boss" below). It's organized around "what you say to it, and what it does."
> It reflects the version released on 2026-10-07.

**How to use it, in one line**: talk to it the way you would talk to a reliable assistant: "Note this down for me", "I'm meeting Lao Lu tomorrow at 3 pm", "Send me an end-of-day summary at 18:00 every day", "Who should make this PPT?", "What can you do?". No commands to memorize.
