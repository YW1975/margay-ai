# 05 Standing orders and "What can you do?"

## Standing orders (setting rules)

- "**Keep an eye on the budget sheet and tell me if anyone changes it**", "**Remember, tell me first about anything to do with clients**", "I'd like you to show me the weekly report before you send it each time."
- It records **your exact words**, without rewording them. It then recalls these orders in three situations and decides for itself what to do:
  - during the background program's round every 15 minutes;
  - in the morning brief / end-of-day summary;
  - in your everyday conversations with it.
- "What standing orders do I have?", "Pause that one", "Cancel that one", "Resume that one."
- Each "thing to pay attention to" you mentioned during onboarding is also recorded as a standing order.

## "What can you do?"

When you ask "**What can you do?**" or "Can you help me…", or give it a new responsibility ("From now on you're in charge of following up on sales", "Track how work is going across the whole company every day"), Staff doesn't answer from memory. It **first takes an honest inventory**:

- **Can do now**: for example, reaching you over IM, sending the day's plan every morning, timed reminders, the end-of-day summary, checking and adding calendar items, handing work to other assistants… It says exactly how (for example, "sent to your WeCom private chat at 18:00 every day").
- **Needs to be connected first**: it has the capability but one step is missing. It tells you what's missing and what you need to do (scan a QR code, grant permission, tell it what time to send).
- **Can't do**: missing data, permissions, or an underlying capability. It says so clearly and **writes a help request**.

**It won't claim something works when it isn't connected.**

## New responsibilities: self-assess before acting

When you give it a new responsibility, it breaks the request into steps and puts each one under "can do directly / needs to be connected first / I need to upgrade myself / can't do". It **shows you this list first and only starts once you nod**.

## Help requests

For things it can't do, it writes a help request and tells you about it in one line in a private chat:

- **Things that need your help** (granting permission, providing data, making a decision): for example, "I need access credentials for Yunxiao";
- **Things that need development to add a capability**: these go into the development request queue.

Ask "What help requests do you have?" to see them.

## Limits on upgrading itself

- **It can**: create new skills or workflows in its own personal layer (for example, writing a new skill for "summarize everyone's progress every day"), leaving a record each time. Ask "What have you changed about yourself lately?" to see.
- **It can't**: change officially installed skills or Staff's own program. When it finds a problem with those, it puts together a **change proposal** (what to change, why, test results) for you to approve; development then merges it into the official version and installs it. See chapter 06 for details.
